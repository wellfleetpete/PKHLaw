## Key Design Changes

### 1. **Business Card Aesthetic**
- Clean header with your name, title, location, and direct contact
- Single-column, linear flow—no distracting sidebars
- Minimal visual elements, maximum clarity
- Fast scroll (one page, ~3 screens on mobile)

### 2. **Immigration Appeals as Hero Message**
- Lead with your specialty: "Immigration Appeals Specialist"
- Not "we do everything"—show strategic focus
- Immediately conveys expertise and specialization

### 3. **Streamlined Content**
- **About:** ~100 words (concise background)
- **Services:** 3 key areas (not 6)
  - Immigration Appeals (your differentiation)
  - Visa & Green Card Applications
  - Employment-Based Immigration
- **Contact:** Phone, email, simple contact form

### 4. **Visual Refinement**
- Responsive layout that works perfectly on mobile
- Subtle borders and dividers (no heavy ornaments)
- Professional spacing and hierarchy
- Left-aligned accent bars on service cards (simple, elegant)
- Hover effects are minimal (professional, not trendy)

### 5. **Header Design**
- Two-column responsive header
- Left: Name, title, location
- Right: Contact phone and email
- Professional, immediately scannable

### 6. **Color Usage**
- Dark blue-gray from your LinkedIn profile as primary
- Gray accents for secondary information
- Light backgrounds for visual breathing room
- Consistent with your professional brand
