### 3. SUBSTANCE & MESSAGING ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Current Strengths:**
- **Lead message:** "Immigration Appeals Specialist" immediately establishes niche
- **Credential clarity:** No wasted words; every line builds trust
- **Service specificity:** "BIA and Federal Courts" appeals work is crystal clear
- **Global reach established:** "Representing... clients worldwide"

**Content Recommendations:**

#### A. Hero/Intro Section - Consider a Minor Addition
Current: "25+ years representing businesses, employees, and families in U.S. immigration proceedings."

Optional enhancement: "25+ years specializing in immigration appeals. Representing businesses, employees, and families in U.S. immigration proceedings at all levels, from initial petitions through federal court appeals."

*Why:* Subtly emphasizes appeals expertise even in the intro. (This is optional—current version works fine.)

#### B. About Section - Consideration
The about text is strong, but you might add one sentence about approach:

"His practice specializes in immigration appeals and represents clients globally in all aspects of U.S. immigration matters. Peter combines deep legal expertise with a strategic, data-driven approach to complex immigration challenges."

*Why:* LinkedIn profile mentions "Data Analysis" skills; this sentence bridges that credibility signal. (Optional.)

#### C. Services Section - One Minor Tweak
Currently, "Family-Based Immigration" appears as third service. 

**Consider reordering:** Some firms lead with their highest-volume service for conversion. Reorder to match your actual practice priorities:
1. Immigration Appeals (specialty)
2. Employment-Based Visas (likely high value)
3. Family-Based Immigration (or swap based on your practice)

*Impact:* Subtle but affects which service gets most attention.

### 4. USER EXPERIENCE & FORM ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Desktop Experience:**

- Contact form is appropriately placed, simple and uncluttered

**Mobile Experience:**
- Photo displays beautifully on narrow screens
- Text remains fully readable
- No horizontal scrolling or layout breaks
- Form inputs are appropriately sized for touch

**Minor Suggestion:**
Consider adding a small **margin-bottom** between the About section photo/text and the Services section to add breathing room on mobile. Currently the sections feel slightly compressed on phones.

```css
.about {
    padding: 3rem 2rem 4rem; /* Add extra bottom padding on mobile */
}

@media (min-width: 768px) {
    .about {
        padding: 3rem 2rem; /* Revert on desktop */
    }
}
```

### 5. CONVERSION & CONTACT STRATEGY ⭐⭐⭐

**Current Approach:**
- Contact form as the only contact method (no phone/email exposed)
- Simple, 3-field form (name, email, message)
- Clear "Send" button with response time reassurance

**Assessment:**
✅ **Good:** Protects your privacy, filters to serious inquiries
⚠️ **Consider:** Some prospects may want to call or email directly—invisible contact info might lose a few inquiries

**Optional Path:**
You could add a footer-only contact line: "(617) 523-2900 • pkh@pkhlaw.com"—visible only at the bottom, so it doesn't clutter the main sections but is available for those seeking it.

*Pro:* Doesn't change design; adds optional contact pathway
*Con:* Slightly increases exposure

**Recommendation:** Keep current approach (form-only) unless you find you're missing opportunities. The clean approach is intentional and professional.
